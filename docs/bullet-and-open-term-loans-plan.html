ZoloFund · lending products

Bullet and open-term loans

Interest-only exists and works — for a fixed monthly tenure. What is missing is a term expressed as a period: a 15-day advance settled in one payment, and an open pledge that runs until the borrower closes it.

2. The gap, in one sentence

A loan's term is stored as frequency × tenure — an instalment count, never a period. So "15 days" has no expression: tenure: 1, frequency: 'daily' means one payment tomorrow, and tenure: 15 means fifteen daily collections. The product the borrower is actually buying — take money now, hand back principal plus a fixed charge on a named date — cannot be written down.

Everything else the request describes already exists in some form. The fix is one new axis, not a new engine.

3. Proposed model — two independent axes

Today one control (deductionType) conflates what is charged with how the money comes back. Split them:

Term shape ↓   Charge →Deducted upfrontAdded at the endMonthly interest, principal at close
Instalments — n × cadence exists upfront_fixed / _percentage exists emi_flat / emi_floating exists interest_only, monthly only
Bullet — one payment, term in days new ← the "deduct and lend" 15-day advance new ← the "fixed interest at the end" 15-day advance new ← short interest-only with a hard maturity
Open — runs until closed n/a n/a new ← the renewable pledge; reuses the gold accrual

The whole plan is: fill the six empty cells with one new field pair (termType, termDays) and one promoted function (the gold accrual), rather than adding a sixth interest model.

4. Worked examples

A · ₹1,00,000 for 15 days, interest at the end

termType   bullet          termDays 15        charge  flat 3%
start      2026-06-01      maturity 2026-06-16

disbursed          1,00,000     ← borrower receives the full principal
interest            3,000       = 1,00,000 × 3%
schedule           1 row: 2026-06-16 due 1,03,000
totalPayable       1,03,000

Effective cost 3% for 15 days ≈ 73% p.a. — worth surfacing on the form, because "3%" reads cheap next to a monthly rate.

B · The same advance, interest deducted at the door

termType   bullet          termDays 15        charge  3% upfront
disbursed            97,000     ← 1,00,000 − 3,000
schedule           1 row: 2026-06-16 due 1,00,000
totalPayable       1,00,000

Same ₹3,000 in the lender's pocket, but the borrower funds it themselves: 3,000 / 97,000 over 15 days ≈ 75.3% p.a. The two variants are not equivalent and the form should show both yields side by side when the operator switches.

C · Fixed-tenure interest-only — works today

₹5,00,000 · 2% per month · 12 months · due on the 5th

monthlyInterest      10,000      = 5,00,000 × 2%
schedule             12 × 10,000  (interest only)
principal at close  5,00,000      bullet, OUTSIDE the schedule
totalPayable        6,20,000      APR 24%

close in month 7, six dues paid, one unpaid:
  totalDueToClose   5,10,000     = principal + interest already billed
                                   (months 8-12 are NOT charged)

Turn on interest_only_enabled and this is available now. No code needed.

D · Open-ended interest-only — new

₹2,00,000 · 2.5% per month · no end date
monthlyInterest       5,000

borrower closes after 3 months and 8 days:
  rule = full        4 months charged → 20,000  → redemption 2,20,000
  rule = prorated    3 + 8/30 = 3.267 → 16,333  → redemption 2,16,333

part-payment of 50,000 in month 2:
  outstandingPrincipal  1,50,000
  monthly interest from then on  3,750

This is pledgeInterestDue() exactly as gold already runs it — promoted out of lib/gold/ and made available to any lending module. The partial-month rule becomes a tenant setting; full matches the market norm ("one month's interest for 23 days").

E · Renewal at maturity

Example C matures. Interest was serviced; the principal was not.

renew → old loan closed, new loan created:
  principal        5,00,000     carried
  interestRate     2%/month     carried   ← today's route DROPS this
  fresh schedule   12 × 10,000
  renewedFromId              ← today: stuffed into voucherRef

if 10,000 interest is still unpaid, two paths, operator's explicit choice:
  a. collect it first, then renew
  b. capitalise it: new principal 5,10,000, monthly interest 10,200

Silently doing (b) is how a book quietly compounds. It must be a checkbox with an audit trail, never the default.

5. Defects found in the existing renew route

Renewal closes a loan with unpaid dues and moves no money. app/api/v1/loans/[id]/renew/route.ts sets the old loan to closed unconditionally — arrears and their penalty context disappear from the book. It also copies perInstalment but not interestRate, outstandingPrincipal or totalPayable, so renewing an interest-only loan produces one whose monthly interest computes to zero. No wallet float moves, no GL entry is posted, and the link to the previous contract lives in voucherRef as a string.

Phase 3 fixes these regardless of which term shapes are approved — an interest-only renewal is unusable until it lands.

6. Implementation

  1. No code · today

    Turn on what already ships

    Set interest_only_enabled = '1' for the tenant (Settings → Features), originate example C, service it with a part-payment, close it. This confirms the existing half and gives the team the vocabulary before anything is built.

  2. Core change · unlocks examples A and B

    Bullet term — a period, one payment

    Add termType (scheduled | bullet | open, default scheduled) and termDays to Loan and LoanPackage. Reuse the existing endDate as the maturity — no new date column. calculateLoanPreview gains the shape: for bullet, tenure is 1 and the schedule is a single row dated start + termDays, carrying the whole payable. Every charge model keeps working unchanged behind it, which is what makes A and B one feature rather than two.

    • lib/loanCalculator.ts · lib/utils.ts (calculateInstalmentDates, calculateEndDate)
    • app/api/v1/loans/route.ts — accept and validate the pair (and validate frequency, which is currently unchecked)
    • LoanForm.tsx — a Term control: cadence + count, or days-to-maturity
    • prisma migration · tests/calc cases · docs/CALCULATION_LOGIC.md §2, §4

  3. Depends on 2 · unlocks example D

    Open term — accrual instead of a schedule

    Promote lib/gold/pledgeInterest.ts to a shared lib/interest/accrual.ts (gold keeps importing it, unchanged). An open interest-only loan is originated with no schedule rows; a monthly cron mints each interest due as it falls, idempotent per (loanId, period), so the agent worklist and the collection flow keep working untouched. /loans/[id]/principal computes interest by accrual rather than by summing instalments. Partial-month rule becomes a tenant setting, defaulting to full.

    • lib/interest/accrual.ts (promoted) · lib/interestOnly.ts
    • app/api/cron/accrue-interest-only/route.ts (new)
    • app/api/v1/loans/[id]/principal/route.ts
    • MONEY-3 amended: interest-only requires a monthly cadence or an open term

  4. Independent of 2 and 3 · fixes live defects

    Renewal that carries the contract

    Carry interestRate, outstandingPrincipal and totalPayable. Add renewedFromId. Refuse to renew over unsettled arrears unless the operator explicitly capitalises them, and record which was chosen. Post the accounting: a renewal that advances fresh cash is a disbursement and must move float and hit the GL.

    • app/api/v1/loans/[id]/renew/route.ts · prisma migration · e2e case

  5. Small · after 2

    Products, so it is configured once

    Extend LoanPackage with the term pair so a branch defines "15-day bridge @ 3%" once and originates from it, instead of retyping terms per loan.

8. Decisions needed before phase 2

  1. Term unit for bullet loans — days only, or days and months? Days alone covers "15 days" and "3 months" (90); months avoid the 28/30/31 argument on a 3-month product.
  2. Partial-month rule default — full (a part month bills as a whole one, the market norm and gold's current default) or prorated. Per tenant, or per loan?
  3. Penalty on a bullet loan past maturity — the existing per-day accrual, a flat overdue charge, or rolled interest?
  4. Open-term ceiling — does an open loan run indefinitely, or auto-flag for review after N months? RBI classification keeps running either way.
  5. Renewal with arrears — block until cleared, or allow capitalising into the new principal with an explicit tick?
  6. Scope — micro-lending only, or every lending module (autofinance, goldloan, property, productfinance) at once? The change is in shared code, so all-modules costs little more than one.
