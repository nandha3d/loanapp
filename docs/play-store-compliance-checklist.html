Google Play Compliance & App Launch Guide

Detailed verification checklist demonstrating why ZoloFund is an Internal Field-Tracking & Business ERP Tool, and how to configure your Google Play Console submission to guarantee smooth approval.

Key Clarification: Is this app a "Finance / Loan" product?

NO. For Google Play policy purposes, ZoloFund is an Enterprise Field-Operations & Bookkeeping Software used exclusively by registered staff (loan officers, field collection agents, and branch managers). It does not lend money, advertise loans, or disburse cash to consumers.

1. App Categorization & Declarations
Critical Setup
Set App Category to "Business" (NOT Finance)
In Play Console → Store settings → App category, select Business or Productivity. Do NOT select "Finance" or "Personal Loans". Selecting "Personal Loans" triggers consumer lending license audits and RBI partner requirements that do not apply to internal ERP tools.
Complete "Financial Features" Policy Declaration Correctly
In App content → Financial features declaration form: Select "My app doesn't provide any financial features or personal loans directly to consumers" or "Internal business/ERP tool for enterprise accounting". Use the statement below if a descriptive text box is provided:
This application is a B2B enterprise SaaS and field-operations management tool used exclusively by authorized field agents and administrative staff of registered businesses for route planning, attendance tracking, and internal collection recording. The application does not provide, broker, facilitate, or disburse loans to the general public or consumers.
2. Google Reviewer App Access (Demo Credentials)
Mandatory
Provide Unrestricted Demo Credentials in App Access
Google reviewers cannot create accounts or receive SMS OTPs. Go to Play Console → App content → App access, choose "All or some functionality is restricted", click "Add instructions", and provide:
Login Instructions for Google Play Reviewers: 1. Open the app to the Sign-in screen. 2. If prompted for Server / Tenant, enter: muraibuiness.in (or default) 3. Username / Email: [YOUR_DEMO_AGENT_USERNAME] 4. Password: [YOUR_DEMO_AGENT_PASSWORD] 5. Tap 'Sign In' to access the Field Agent Dashboard, Collection Routes, and Customer Ledgers. Note: 2FA and SMS verification are disabled on this review account.
3. Sensitive Permissions & Location Declaration
Requires Review
Declare Background Location for Agent Field Route Tracking
The mobile app uses location to map customer collection pins and track field agent travel routes. In App content → Location permissions, declare that location is required for field agent tracking with an ongoing foreground notification.
Purpose: Field agent collection route tracking and customer visit verification. The app collects location data only during active agent duty hours to track travel routes between customer collection stops and verify on-site visits, supported by a persistent foreground notification.

5. Privacy Policy & Data Safety Form
Verification
Submit Valid Public Privacy Policy URL
In App content → Privacy policy, provide your live public privacy URL (e.g. https://muraibuiness.in/privacy). The policy must disclose that:
  • GPS location is collected strictly for agent route tracking and collection verification.
  • No personal customer or agent data is sold or shared with third-party advertisers.
  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS / TLS) and securely stored.
