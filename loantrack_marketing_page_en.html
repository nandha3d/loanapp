Simple daily workflow

Your team knows what to do next.

LoanTrack keeps owners, admins, agents, and borrowers aligned with separate portals and a clean approval trail.

1

Register the customer

Capture documents, photos, guarantors, income details, bureau checks, and KYC status.

2

Create the loan

Choose product, interest model, tenure, schedule, collateral, penalty rules, and collection frequency.

3

Collect with proof

Agents see their route, collect payments, add GPS proof, scan QR codes, and submit cash handovers.

4

Review and report

Admins approve exceptions, track overdue accounts, export reports, and monitor NPA movement.
