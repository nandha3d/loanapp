  2. GL-165 A month-end pledge does not gain or lose a month in February

    Error: elapsedMonthsDays walks its anchor with setMonth, which overflows a short month: Jan 31 + 1 month lands on Mar 3, the walk-back then lands on Feb 3, and the pledge is measured from the 3rd rather than from the day the metal came in

  3. GL-207 Advancing a month-end date does not skip a month
