# ZoloFund — Complete Product Overview

> **The all-in-one lending management platform for microfinance companies, auto finance firms, and chit fund operators.**

---

## What Is ZoloFund?

ZoloFund is a complete business management software designed for lending companies of all sizes. Whether you run a small neighborhood lending operation or a large multi-branch financial firm, ZoloFund gives you everything you need to manage your customers, loans, collections, and team — all in one place.

It works on **web browsers** (laptops, desktops) and as a dedicated **mobile app** on Android and iOS phones, so your team can work from anywhere.

---

## Who Is It For?

ZoloFund serves three types of lending businesses:

| Business Type | What It Covers |
|---|---|
| **Microfinance / Moneylenders** | Group and individual small loans with daily, weekly, or monthly repayment |
| **Auto Finance Companies** | Vehicle-backed loans with full vehicle document management |
| **Chit Fund Operators** | Rotating savings groups (chit groups) with auction and dividend management |

All three can be managed from the same platform — no need for separate software.

---

## Who Uses It Inside Your Company?

ZoloFund has separate logins and dashboards for every type of person in your team:

- **Business Owner / Admin** — Full control over every loan, customer, agent, and report in your company
- **Field Collection Agents** — A simple mobile app to collect payments, view their daily route, and add new customers
- **Borrowers** — A private web portal where customers can view their own loan details and approve payments
- **Affiliate Partners** — An external dashboard for referral partners to track their leads and earnings

# Features & Modules

---

## 1. Customer Management

Everything you need to maintain a professional, organized customer database.

- **Complete customer profiles** — name, phone, PAN, Aadhaar, address, income, business details, and photos
- **Document uploads** — store KYC documents (ID proofs, address proofs) securely in the cloud
- **Aadhaar OTP verification** — verify a customer's identity digitally in seconds
- **Video KYC** — conduct a video-based identity check remotely
- **Guarantors** — attach multiple guarantors to any customer, each with their own photo and details
- **Security cheques** — record and track up to 5 post-dated security cheques per customer
- **Credit Score** — every customer automatically gets a score (300–850) based on their repayment history, rated from *Excellent* to *Very Poor*
- **Customer status tracking** — know at a glance if a customer is active, overdue, closed, or blacklisted
- **Approval workflow** — field agents can create customers; the admin reviews and approves before any loan is issued

---

## 2. Loan Management

Full control over how you structure, issue, and manage loans.

- **Flexible interest types** — choose from 4 interest calculation methods (upfront flat, upfront percentage, EMI flat, or reducing balance)
- **Multiple repayment schedules** — Daily · Weekly · Every Two Weeks · Monthly
- **Live loan calculator** — see the exact disbursed amount, total repayable, and per-instalment amount before issuing the loan
- **Loan codes** — each loan gets a unique automatically-generated code (e.g., DL0001 for daily, ML0001 for monthly)
- **Full repayment schedule** — view the complete instalment plan on screen
- **Loan restructuring** — if a customer is struggling, spread their overdue amount across remaining instalments without creating a new loan
- **Loan closing options:**
  - Normal closure after full repayment
  - Renewal (issue a new loan on top)
  - Pre-closure (customer pays early)
  - **Foreclosure** — settle with a calculated final amount, including any outstanding balance, penalties, and applicable discounts
- **PDF Loan Statement** — generate and download a full loan statement at any time
- **PDF Receipt** — a printable payment receipt for every collection

---

## 3. Collection Management

The heart of your daily operations — collecting repayments made simple and accurate.

- **Daily collection list** — agents see exactly who they need to collect from today
- **Filter and search** — find any customer's collection by route, date, overdue status, or name
- **Smart payment distribution** — when a customer pays, the system automatically applies the money to the oldest overdue instalments first, so nothing slips through
- **Payment methods** — Cash · UPI · Cheque · Bank Transfer
- **GPS location stamp** — every payment collection is tagged with the exact location where it was made, creating a verified record
- **Location verification** — the system checks if the collection was made near the customer's address and flags any discrepancies
- **Overdue settlement** — agents can see a breakdown of every missed instalment and collect for all of them in one go
- **Cash handover workflow** — agents hand over collected cash to the admin digitally; both sides confirm the handover on the app
- **UPI QR code display** — agents can show the company's UPI QR code on their phone screen for instant digital payments
- **Edit window** — a short time window (set by admin) during which agents can correct a collection entry if they made a mistake
- **No duplicate payments** — the system prevents the same payment from being recorded twice, even by accident
- **Auto-mark missed** — at midnight, any unpaid instalments are automatically flagged as missed, keeping your records accurate

---

## 4. Penalty Management

Automatically track and manage late payment penalties.

- **Automatic penalty accrual** — penalties are calculated and added per day after a grace period
- **Configurable rules** — set your own grace period, daily penalty amount, and a maximum penalty cap
- **Partial or full settlement** — collect penalty in part or in full
- **Penalty waiver** — admins can waive a penalty with a reason note (tracked in the audit log)
- **Full penalty history** — every penalty action is recorded with timestamps

Everything that needs a second pair of eyes goes through a formal approval workflow:

- New customer registrations (created by agents)
- Changes to customer details (with a before/after comparison view)
- Loan edits
- Collection corrections
- Cash handover confirmations
- New branch creations

Admins see a clear dashboard of everything pending their review — approve or reject with notes in one click.

## 6. Chit Fund Management

A complete chit fund operations module built into the same platform.

- **Create chit groups** — set the number of members, monthly contribution, total value, duration, and commission percentage
- **Automated setup** — the system generates all member subscriptions and monthly auction slots automatically
- **Auction recording** — record each month's auction winner; the system auto-calculates the bid discount, commission, and dividend for each member
- **Dividend redistribution** — the system automatically works out how much each non-winning member receives as dividend
- **Member payments** — record monthly contributions (full or partial) for each member
- **Chit Group Statement PDF** — download a complete statement for any chit group
- **Dashboard summary** — see all active groups, completed groups, and total members at a glance

## 13. Payment Proof System

A fraud-proof way to verify payments between agents and customers.

**Photo Proof (with customer approval):**
1. Agent takes a photo at the time of collection
2. The customer gets notified on their borrower portal
3. The customer reviews and approves or rejects the payment
4. Only after approval is the payment recorded on the loan

**QR Code Proof (instant verification):**
1. Customer logs into their portal and generates a single-use QR code for their instalment
2. Agent scans the QR code on their phone
3. Payment is instantly verified and recorded — no approval needed
4. Both parties receive a confirmation notification

## 16. NPA Classification

Stay compliant with RBI norms automatically.

ZoloFund automatically classifies every loan account by its asset quality every night:

| Classification | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Standard | All payments on time |
| SMA-0 | 1–30 days overdue |
| SMA-1 | 31–60 days overdue |
| SMA-2 | 61–90 days overdue |
| Sub-Standard | 91 days – 1 year overdue |
| Doubtful | 1–3 years overdue |
| Loss | Unrecoverable |

- Automatic nightly classification — no manual work needed
- Provisioning calculator included
- Full upgrade/downgrade history tracked

## 21. Security & Data Protection

Your business data is protected at every level.

- Every company's data is completely isolated — no crossover between tenants
- Role-based access — everyone only sees what their role allows
